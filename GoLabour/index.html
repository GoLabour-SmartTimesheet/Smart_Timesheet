GoLabour on your phone

On iPhone

  1. Open this link in Safari.
  2. Tap the page menu or Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

On Android

Open the link in Chrome. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app from the browser menu.

Open the app online once to prepare offline access.

You can fill in and sign offline after the app is ready. Sharing through WhatsApp needs an internet connection.

Save the signed photo or PDF before closing the page. Each fresh visit starts a new timesheet.
