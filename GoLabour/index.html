GoLabour invoice & payment instructions

Email your invoice to invoice@golabour.com.au.

Payments are processed every Wednesday for invoices received by Sunday.

  • Include your name, ABN and bank account.
  • Include job name, job date and total amount.
  • Breaks are unpaid.
  • A timesheet is not an invoice.

**Submit no later than Sunday, 11:59 pm. Invoices sent after Sunday will be paid in the next payment cycle.

**Returned for correction? Resubmit the corrected invoice no later than Tuesday, 11:59 pm to be paid on Thursday.
